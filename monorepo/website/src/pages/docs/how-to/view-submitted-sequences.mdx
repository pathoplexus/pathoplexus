You can easily view sequences that are awaiting approval/release, or that have been released.

To do this, click on 'Browse database' on the landing page or the 'Organisms' drop-down menu in the top right, then select the pathogen you're interested in. To see sequences that are awaiting approval click 'Submit sequences' in the top-left of the page, followed by 'Review' (you can then [edit](/docs/how-to/edit-submissions) and [approve](/docs/how-to/approve-submissions) these sequences). To see sequences that have been released by your group, click 'View'.
